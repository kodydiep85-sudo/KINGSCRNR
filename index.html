Kings Corner

Empty your hand first. Build down in alternating colors, and Kings take the corners.

Play with friends

Everyone plays on their own phone or computer. One person hosts and shares a 4-letter code. Up to 4 players.

The game keeps going even if someone closes the page. They can come back with the same code.

Play the computer

You against up to three computer players.

Game code

    Your hand
    How to play
    • Empty your hand first to win. You draw one card automatically at the start of each turn.
    • Build down in alternating colors: a black 8 goes on a red 9. Aces are low and end a pile.
    • Kings go only in the four corner spots. If you hold a King and a corner is open, you must play it.
    • Tap a card, then tap a glowing pile. Tap a selected card again to play it on the first spot it fits.
    • To move a whole pile, tap the pile, then tap where it goes. Its bottom card has to fit the target's top card.
    • An empty side spot (north, east, south, west) takes any card except a King.
    • If the stock runs out and nobody can move for a full round, the fewest cards wins.
    • Online: if a player drops out, the game plays their turns for them until they rejoin from the same device.
